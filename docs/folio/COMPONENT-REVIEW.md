## Findings and repair evidence

### Resolved P2: The isolated Dialog story was an unpadded full-height inspector

Opening `components-studio-primitives--dialog` produces a full-height inspector slab despite containing only a short confirmation. On phone the dialog occupies exactly 390×844, has `padding: 0px`, and its heading begins at x1/y3. Close and Approve buttons abut the left edge and each other. At desktop the slab is approximately 800px wide, begins at y0, and retains the same flush content. Actual light and dark screenshots both show this.

The original story passed `className="cast-inspector"` to DialogSurface and put a plain Panel directly inside it. The cast inspector composition expects its own padded sections; the isolated story supplied none. This was a composition/review-surface defect, not evidence that the production cast inspector had the same issue. A green focus-trap test did not approve its rendered layout.

The implementation owner replaced that misuse with shared `.dialog-backdrop`, `.dialog-card` and `.dialog-actions` classes. Independent live recheck measured the light phone dialog at x16/y307.77, width358/height228.45, with 24px padding; the title now starts at x41/y335.77. Dark desktop measured x460/y343.05, width520/height213.90, with 24px padding. Screenshots showed a centered compact confirmation, readable margins and separated buttons in both reviewed states. In each state, manual opening focused the dialog, Tab reached Close review, and Escape restored the trigger. The local repair resolves the observed defect; these measurements describe the uncommitted repair after the original audited revision.

### Resolved P3: Dark isolated previews were surrounded by a light canvas

The scoped dark PresentationRoot is correct, but the Storybook body stays light outside it. Most short stories appear as a dark rectangle on a large pale field. This does not demonstrate a production appearance leak—the App and help portal render correctly—but it weakens dark-mode review and makes the component boundary look accidental. Frame isolated stories with an appearance-matched review canvas without changing the sibling-root or consumer scoping contract.

The implementation owner added Storybook-only `preview.css`, imported by `.storybook/preview.ts`. IAB became unavailable, so an explicitly authorized isolated headless Chromium fallback performed the independent local-development recheck. A body-only first repair measured dark on body but actual screenshots still showed a pale lower canvas: the short body left the html background exposed. The corrected `:is(html, body)` rule requires a dark root and excludes any light root. Actual [Actions](evidence/components-repairs/components-studio-primitives--actions-canvas-repaired.png) and [Introduction](evidence/components-repairs/components-workflow-compositions--introduction-canvas-repaired.png) screenshots now show a charcoal canvas throughout. Both html/body measure `rgb(24, 30, 32)`.

The [mixed appearance siblings](evidence/components-repairs/foundations-presentation-contract--appearance-siblings-canvas-repaired.png) retain light html/body `rgb(244, 241, 235)` and independent light/dark roots with corresponding native color schemes. Their action colors remain `rgb(185, 70, 48)` and `rgb(240, 160, 128)`. The separate palette story also retains baseline/proposal/host action colors `rgb(185, 70, 48)`, `rgb(35, 85, 72)` and `rgb(47, 64, 110)`. The observed framing defect is resolved without changing production product CSS.

## Judgment and limits

The shared primitives support a coherent warm workshop identity. Manuscript typography is reserved for source and bibliographic/empty-state content; action text and working headings stay practical. Semantic status words, restrained selected surfaces, and visible focus are more useful than ornamental effects. The observed dialog composition, dark story canvas and Dialogue result overlay defects have been repaired and rendered again.

This review does not grant an S grade, claim a pristine UI, or establish user approval. Hover was not systematically exercised for every primitive, and arbitrary long labels, all disabled-choice variants, forced-colors, OS appearance changes, preference persistence, screen-reader audio output and reduced-motion behavior were not independently tested in this pass. The existing automated checks and color probes support their specific contracts only. Synthetic story audio does not establish listening quality.
