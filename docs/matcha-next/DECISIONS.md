# Decisions with workflow consequences

| Problem and credible options | Decision | Evidence and trade-off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voice audition comparison required a separate “Approve Audition N” action after choosing. Leaving the second action preserves the old flow; applying on selection shortens it. | Clicking an audition card selects its exact revision immediately in the draft. A single Play/Stop control handles listening independently. **New voice** switches to the existing voice-creation controls; generating still requires its explicit action. Cast-stage acceptance remains separate and authoritative. | Cast interaction fixtures and focused tests verify revision selection, playback, New voice/Back and stage acceptance. An accidental card choice is reversible by choosing another audition before accepting Cast. |
| Cast tiles repeated a separate Listen footer, line count and readiness sentence on every card. Making the whole tile play would hide the inspector action; keeping two targets preserves both tasks. | The initial portrait is the play/pause button and swaps to a small sound motif while active. The name opens auditions. The roster marks chosen or required voices with a slim edge and tiny check or exclamation mark, while accessible descriptions and the workbench summary carry full state. | Desktop/phone and reduced-motion browser checks verify the split controls and state restoration. The small play cue and page-level instruction aid discovery; line count remains available in the inspector. |
| Accepted Review “Change settings” returned to a sparse manual Scan CTA. Always showing the CTA is explicit; automatically scanning removes a redundant step. | That intentional return launches the existing nonpublishing Review candidate scan once and shows in-page progress. Initial entry still exposes an explicit Scan action. Failure leaves Retry; no accepted review is replaced until ordinary acceptance. | App return/failure tests and the existing `process_review(... publish=False)` backend boundary. This spends local processing upon a clear editing action; no new provider route or publication authority is added. |
| Cast’s three native confirmations bypassed the product dialog and had inconsistent focus. An accepted Cast was then copied into the editor even though backend edits require a candidate, and it opened Cast when the common next task was voice review. | Use `DialogSurface` with explicit cancel/continue actions and focus restoration. Confirming replacement automatically prepares an editable candidate and opens Voices; Edit cast remains available there. A saved newer draft opens directly. | Browser keyboard/Escape, direct Voices return, edit navigation, failure recovery and backend rebase checks. Preparing a candidate may perform local processing; the accepted Cast remains active until replacement acceptance. |
| The fixed-color decoration could contaminate custom host palettes or distract from content. | Mark custom semantic `PresentationRoot` palettes and show the new canopy only in the canonical studio. It sits behind content and moves by a few pixels; reduced-motion stops it. | Eight rendered mode/width/motion checks verify decoded art, no overflow and palette isolation. The image adds about 300 KB to the canonical style package. |
