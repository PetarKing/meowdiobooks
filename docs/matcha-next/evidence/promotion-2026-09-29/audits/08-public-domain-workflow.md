## Limits and resolved environment gap

Initially the three optional decoder tests failed because `mlx_audio` was absent. After the parent authorized installing the locked optional runtime, `uv sync --locked --extra mlx-runtime` succeeded and the full backend run passed all 486 tests. The lockfile remains unchanged. Installation did not download LLM, TTS or ASR weights, and the probes made no paid-provider requests or private-book egress.

No model directories are installed in this worktree. Fully local and remote AI speaker-attribution accuracy, voice naturalness, and listening defects were therefore not scored. `narrate` intentionally leaves unresolved quotes routed to Narrator; the parser candidate counts must not be interpreted as successful speaker attribution. Broader UI navigation, appearance, and audition race checks belong to the other independent audits.
