# Sanctuary canopy

`frontend/src/assets/brand/sanctuary-canopy.webp` is an original decorative illustration generated with the built-in imagegen tool for this local UI candidate. The tool produced a 1536 × 1024 transparent PNG; the selected output was converted with `cwebp -q 84 -m 6 -alpha_q 100` to a 292 KB WebP while retaining transparency. It depicts imagined pine, cherry blossoms and lanterns, without text, a named shrine or religious emblem. The existing reading-cat mark and sanctuary illustration remain the product's identity.

Prompt: “Create one premium decorative bitmap asset for a Japanese-inspired fantasy audiobook studio UI. A cutout ornamental upper-right corner canopy: hand-painted deep olive pine needles and soft matcha leaves arch inward from the top right, interwoven with cherry blossoms in blush pink and wine red; two tiny warm-gold paper lanterns nestled in the foliage and a few loose petals drifting down toward the lower left. Exquisite illustrated book-cover detail, subtle gold ink accents, luminous but restrained, stylistically compatible with an opulent illustrated temple garden and a whimsical orange reading-cat mascot. Keep the majority of the bottom and left empty/transparent so the art can sit behind UI with no content obscured. No cat, no shrine symbol, no lettering, no words, no border, no scene background, no rectangular vignette. Actual transparent background, clean alpha edges. Wide landscape corner composition.”

`styles/atmosphere.css` places it as a washed-out background at the upper right of canonical working stages. It carries no labels or hit targets. A slow, 8–10px position sway is decorative; `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` stops it. A custom `PresentationRoot` semantic palette marks its visual root and excludes the fixed-color artwork. The canvas/color variables still come from the host. The 8-case browser check in [evidence/atmosphere](evidence/atmosphere/report.json) confirmed the asset decoded, no overflow or API requests, custom-palette isolation, and the motion setting.
